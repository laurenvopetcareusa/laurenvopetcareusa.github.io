Welcome to **Laurenvo Pet Care USA** — our new home on GitHub Pages, and the fifth site in the Laurenvo blog family.

## What you'll find here

Practical, honest guides for pet owners across the United States:

- **Dogs** — care, training, gear and safety, plus breed-by-breed guides.
- **Cats** — care, behaviour and gear, plus breed-by-breed guides.
- **Aquarium** — freshwater setup, water care and beginner fish species.

New guides land here every Sunday, alongside our other blogs on [Blogger](https://laurenvopetcareusa.blogspot.com/), [WordPress.com](https://laurenvopetcareusa.wordpress.com/), [Substack](https://laurenvopetcareusa.substack.com/) and [Tumblr](https://laurenvopetcareusa.tumblr.com/). Each site carries its own unique articles — never word-for-word copies.

## Why GitHub Pages?

Because it's fast, free and fully ours. No ads, no platform rules changing under our feet — just a clean, quick blog that loads the same on any device.

## Start with the shop

If you're setting up for a new pet, the [Laurenvo shop](https://www.laurenvo.com/) carries beds, toys, collars, aquarium gear and more for dogs, cats and aquarium pets across the USA.

Thanks for stopping by — the first real guides arrive this Sunday.
