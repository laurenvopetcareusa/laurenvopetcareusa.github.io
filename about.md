Laurenvo Pet Care USA is the blog of [Laurenvo](https://www.laurenvo.com/), a US pet supplies store. We write practical, honest guides for dog owners, cat owners and freshwater aquarium keepers across the United States.

## What we write about

- **Dogs** — care, training, gear and safety guides, plus breed-by-breed care guides.
- **Cats** — care, behaviour, gear and safety guides, plus breed-by-breed care guides.
- **Aquarium** — freshwater setup, water care and fish species guides for beginners.

Every guide is written to be genuinely useful: clear answers first, real numbers with sources, and links to the gear that actually helps — on our store when it's relevant, and to trusted sources when it isn't.

## Our other blogs

Laurenvo Pet Care USA also publishes on [Blogger](https://laurenvopetcareusa.blogspot.com/), [WordPress.com](https://laurenvopetcareusa.wordpress.com/), [Substack](https://laurenvopetcareusa.substack.com/) and [Tumblr](https://laurenvopetcareusa.tumblr.com/). Each site carries its own unique guides.
